# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — OPTYO

## Company Overview

**OPTYO** is a performance marketing agency specializing in the fitness, sports, and wellness industries. We help early-stage brands build consistent revenue and profitable growth through conversion-optimized websites, high-performing email campaigns, and scalable paid ad strategies.

---

## Who We're Built For

**Target:** CEOs and Founders of early-stage brands in the fitness, health, and wellness space.

**Company size:** 1–50 employees

**Stage:** Early-stage — past idea validation, generating some revenue, but not yet at scale. They know their product works, they just need a proven system to grow it.

---

## The Ideal Customer

### Demographics
- **Title:** CEO, Founder, Co-Founder
- **Industry:** Fitness, health, wellness (equipment, supplements, apparel, digital products, services)
- **Team size:** 1–50 employees
- **Revenue stage:** Pre-scale — typically $0–$5M ARR, looking to break through to their next growth tier

### Psychographics
- Wears multiple hats — they're the visionary, the operator, and often the marketer all at once
- Has tried running ads or sending emails themselves (or with a generalist agency) and hit a ceiling
- Frustrated by agencies that don't understand their industry or can't show ROI
- Motivated by building something real — a brand that lasts, not just a quick cash grab
- Values directness — they don't want fluff, they want results and a clear path to get there

### Pain Points
- Revenue is inconsistent — some months are great, others are dead
- Paid ads are burning budget without a clear return
- Email list is growing but not converting
- Website traffic doesn't translate to sales
- Don't have the in-house expertise to run sophisticated marketing at scale
- Generic agencies don't get the fitness/wellness space

### What They're Looking For
- A partner who speaks their language and understands their customer
- A clear, repeatable system to generate revenue — not one-off campaigns
- Proof it works (not promises — case studies from brands like theirs)
- Someone who handles execution so they can focus on building the business

---

## What OPTYO Brings

We build three core growth levers that work together:

1. **Conversion-optimized websites** — sites that turn visitors into buyers, not just look good
2. **High-performing email sequences and campaigns** — automated and broadcast emails that drive consistent revenue from your list
3. **Scalable paid ad strategies** — Meta, Google, and other channels built to scale profitably, not just spend

The result: consistent revenue, profitable growth, and a marketing engine you don't have to babysit.

## Disqualifiers (Who We're NOT for)

- Brands outside fitness, health, or wellness
- Founders who want to stay fully hands-on with marketing execution
- Companies looking for one-time projects with no interest in ongoing growth
- Businesses with no product-market fit or no existing customers
- Brands expecting overnight results with no willingness to invest in a real strategy

---

## Communication Style

**Direct but casual.** We talk to founders like people — no corporate jargon, no fluff. We say what we mean, back it up with data, and keep it moving. The goal is clarity and results, not impressive-sounding decks.
