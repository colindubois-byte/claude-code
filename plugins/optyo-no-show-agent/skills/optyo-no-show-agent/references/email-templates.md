# Approved email templates

These were written and approved by the OPTYO team. Fill in the [brackets] only. No em dashes, no arrows.

Contents: 1 Confirmation, 2 Move-up, 3 Case study, 4 Day-before, 5 Day-of, 6 Booker's DM, 7 Reschedule reply.

---

## 1. Confirmation

Drafted as soon as a first discovery call is found.

**Subject:** Confirmed: OPTYO x [Brand] call on [Day, Date]

Hi [First name],

Thanks for booking time with us. Here are the details:

**When:** [Day, Month Date] at [Time] [their time zone]
**Where:** Google Meet, [Meet link]
**Who:** [OPTYO attendees from the invite]

On the call, we'll learn more about [Brand], where you want to grow, and if OPTYO is the right growth partner for where you are at.

Could you reply "confirmed" so I know this reached you?

Talk soon,
[Sender's first name]

**Same-day, next-day and 2-business-day calls:** add this line before the sign-off:

In the meantime, here's how we helped [Case study brand], a [category] brand, [headline result]: [case study link]

**Next-day and 2-business-day calls only:** also add the discussion areas from template 4 ("Here's what we'll cover:" plus the three bullets) after the "Who" block. If the call is tomorrow, the subject says "call tomorrow, [Date]".

---

## 2. Move-up

Only for calls more than 3 business days out. Drafted the next business day after the confirmation.

Find a real open slot on the sender's Google Calendar in the next 1 to 2 business days, during normal working hours in both the sender's and the prospect's time zones, the same length as the booked call. If there's no real opening, skip this email. Never make up a time.

**Subject:** Earlier time for our call?

Hi [First name],

Quick one: an opening came up on [Day] at [Time] [their time zone]. If you'd like to get started sooner, we can move our call to then.

Or if your original time on [Original day, date] works best, no problem at all, we'll see you then.

Let me know if you'd like to move it or keep our original time.

Best,
[Sender's first name]

## 4. Day-before

Drafted the business day before the call. If that's not literally the day before (for example, Friday for a Monday call), replace "tomorrow" with "on [Day]" everywhere.

**Subject:** Tomorrow: OPTYO x [Brand] at [Time]

Hi [First name],

Looking forward to our call tomorrow at [Time] [their time zone]. Here's the link: [Meet link]

Here's what we'll cover:

- Where [Brand] is today: revenue, sales channels and your current marketing setup
- Your biggest opportunities and what's holding growth back
- What the next 6 to 12 months could look like with the right plan

One question before we talk: what's one part of your DTC business that feels like it's not working as well as it should, or that you think is underused? We'll make sure to spend time on it.

And if this time no longer works, just reply and we'll find a better one.

See you tomorrow,
[Sender's first name]

---

## 5. Day-of

Drafted the evening of the business day before the call. Same-day calls: drafted right away, alert says to send about 1 hour before.

**Subject:** See you today at [Time]

Hi [First name],

Just a reminder that we're meeting today at [Time] [their time zone]. Here's the link: [Meet link]

Talk soon,
[Sender's first name]

---

## 6. Booker's DM (Instagram or LinkedIn)

Not a Gmail draft. Goes inside the booker's day-before alert for them to copy and send by hand. Only if the prospect's Instagram or LinkedIn is saved in monday.com.

Hey [First name], looking forward to our chat tomorrow at [Time]! Sent the details to your email, but here's the link too: [Meet link]

If there's no first name, start with "Hey!" instead.

---

## 7. Reschedule reply

Drafted as a reply in the same thread when a prospect says the time no longer works. Offer 2 to 3 real open slots from the sender's calendar in the next 5 business days, on different days and times of day.

Hi [First name],

No problem at all. Here are a few times that work on our end:

- [Day, Date] at [Time] [their time zone]
- [Day, Date] at [Time] [their time zone]
- [Day, Date] at [Time] [their time zone]

Let me know which one works best, or suggest another time and we'll make it happen.

Talk soon,
[Sender's first name]
