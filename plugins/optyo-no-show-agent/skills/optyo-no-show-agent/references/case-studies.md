## Matching rules

Work out what the prospect sells (category field first, website if the field is blank or unclear), then:

1. **Golf** (clubs, grips, golf apparel, golf accessories, golf tech, "PGA Show"): Golf group. Lead with the closest one: Stick Grips for accessories and gear, Golfjoy for clubs, apparel and lifestyle brands.
2. **Closest neighbor:**
   - Other club and racquet sports (pickleball, tennis, padel): Golf group.
   - Gym and training gear, home gym, fitness equipment, boxing and combat gear, pilates and yoga equipment: Fitness group. MX Select for big-ticket equipment, Heroboard or PureTorque for smaller training products.
   - Wellness, recovery, sauna, cold plunge, sleep, supplements, nutrition, hydration, energy products: Peak Wellness.
3. **No clear match** (general apparel, team sports merch, outdoors, beverages, anything else): fallback is Peak Wellness plus MX Select, because they show OPTYO's biggest numbers.

If what the brand sells can't be worked out at all, skip the case study email and say so in the sender's alert so they can pick one.
